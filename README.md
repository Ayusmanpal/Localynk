# LocaLynk
A hyperlocal digital enablement platform that empowers street vendors and micro-businesses to create an online presence, reach nearby customers, and grow through simple onboarding, product listings, and seamless discovery.

India is home to more than 50 million street vendors and micro-business owners, generating over ₹2.5 lakh crore annually. Despite their massive contribution to the economy, nearly 95% of these businesses lack any digital presence. This makes them dependent on physical footfall, resulting in revenue loss due to seasonal demand fluctuations, poor discoverability, fragmented WhatsApp ordering, and payment challenges.

This project aims to build a hyperlocal digital enablement platform that empowers vendors to easily go online, showcase their products or services, and connect with nearby customers in a seamless and affordable way.
